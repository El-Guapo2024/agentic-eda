## PCB editor (PP)
- **PP1** Board texts sit about 0.1 of their size off KiCad's place, are centred whatever their vertical justification, and take the width as the height. M · cosmetic · PARITY-pcb §66, §91; P4, P15
- **PP2** With several items under the cursor, M/R/F/D/Delete take the first (KiCad opens the clarification menu); a text is hit by its box, a rounded, chamfered or custom pad by its rectangle. M · behaviour-differs · PARITY-pcb §87, §91; P22; HANDOFF 11-07
- **PP3** Draw order is approximate: inner copper rises as one, pads and technical layers stay over all copper, a THT pad is drawn once in its side's colour, and the net highlight leaves DRC markers and shadows undimmed. M · cosmetic · PARITY-pcb §90; P28
- **PP4** A footprint carried by Place Footprint snaps to anchors like a move, not to the grid point; E does nothing while it is carried; a motionless click in a placement grid puts it down unturned. S · behaviour-differs · PARITY-pcb §85, §86; P19
- **PP5** Placing or updating from the library drops pads' layer sets, die lengths and fabrication properties (a no-paste pad gets paste), so Compare with Library keeps flagging; Update/Change Footprint skips the library's description, tags, jumper groups, private layers, 3D body, margins, dimensions, zones and tables. L · behaviour-differs · PARITY-pcb §78, §89; P21, P30, F3, F6; GAPS 26
- **PP6** Teardrops are not kept current as you edit (re-run Add All Teardrops) and are not shrunk to other nets' clearance, so they can raise DRC errors KiCad avoids. M · behaviour-differs · PARITY-pcb §66; P1; GAPS 13, 35
- **PP7** Teardrop shapes: straight and one segment only (Curved and two-segment are stored, not built); no track-to-track, custom-pad, padstack-inner-layer or solder-mask teardrops. L · feature-missing · PARITY-pcb §66, §78; P1, P3
- **PP8** Properties panel rows still read-only: a pad's number, backdrill and post-machining, a copper shape's net, a field's Auto Thickness and Locked; no "Edge connector" pad type; a size of 0 is refused. M · feature-missing · PARITY-pcb §67; P2; GAPS 8, 10
- **PP9** No Origins & Axes setting: X and Y always count from the page origin with Y down (KiCad: page, drill/place or grid origin, either axis direction). M · feature-missing · PARITY-pcb §74; P10
- **PP10** Zones: settings are not sticky, E does nothing while drawing a cutout or similar zone, an unfilled pour is picked by a click anywhere inside, no auto-refill, a two-sided zone flips as mirrored, a zone with holes made a polygon is a slit ring. - **PP11** Text Properties has no Italic; a dimension's label has one size, no font, bold, italic, mirror or keep-upright, and is always centred; no width keys, layer change or E while drawing one, and a slanted height step stays on the grid. - **PP12** Vias: per-layer padstack diameters are drawn, routed and checked at the front's size; Draw Via's ghost is not at the snap point, a violating via goes down silently with Allow DRC violations, and its check misses unflashed PTH layers and copper text boxes, tables and dimensions. - **PP14** A board footprint's own dimensions, zones and tables (and locked or unreadable text boxes, images, points, barcodes) stay raw text: not drawn, edited or moved with it. L · feature-missing · PARITY-pcb §84; P17; GAPS 24, 25
